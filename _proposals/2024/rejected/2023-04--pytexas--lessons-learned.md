# Speed Running Engineering: Lessons Learned Going from Zero to CTO

## Abstract

In this talk, I'll unveil key lessons that fueled my career growth in the tech industry from coding novice to CTO at an early-stage startup. Discover the importance of resilience, the impact of project-based learning, and the benefits of learning in public. I'll share insights into seizing opportunities and balancing technical and soft skills, providing a blueprint for those aiming to advance in the tech field. This session is a concise guide for anyone seeking to navigate their tech career with purpose and agility.

## Description

In this talk, I'll share key lessons from my journey transitioning from a coding newbie to being the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at an early-stage healthcare startup. I'll unfold the narrative of my career, marked by resilience, innovative learning approaches, and seizing the right opportunities. You'll hear about the power of project-based learning, which helped me hone my technical skills through real-world challenges, and the unexpected benefits of learning in public, which fostered a deeper understanding and community engagement.

I'll also delve into the crucial balance between technical prowess and soft skills, a blend that is essential for anyone aspiring to lead in the tech industry. This talk aims to provide a blueprint for tech career advancement, highlighting how to navigate the tech world with agility and purpose. Whether you're just starting out or looking to elevate your career, the insights shared will offer both inspiration and practical advice.

Join me for a session filled with personal anecdotes, lessons learned, and strategies that can help guide your path in the tech industry. This talk is more than a personal journey; it's a roadmap for anyone aiming to climb the ladder in the ever-evolving tech landscape.
